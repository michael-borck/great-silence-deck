01The Silence That Isn'T

The Fermi frame — Absence is data.

The counter-argument — Choose which absences matter, then monitor them.

The exercise — Good operators treat absence as an event.

02The Dark Forest

The Liu Cixin frame — Attack surface is the sum of everything observable, not just the software.

The counter-argument — Audit by default, not by accident.

The exercise — One lit window in a dark forest is a signal. It is also a target.

03The Aestivation Hypothesis

The thermodynamics frame — Efficiency is a survival metric.

The counter-argument — Jevons' paradox: efficiency without decisions increases consumption.

The exercise — Report the saving as a number, not a feeling.

04The Zoo Hypothesis

The observation frame — The same data is analytics in one room and surveillance in another.

The counter-argument — Name who is in the enclosure and who holds the clipboard.

The exercise — The last column is usually the shortest.

05The Byzantine Generals

The consensus frame — Name the assumptions or the governance is a hope.

The counter-argument — Agents that share a model share failure modes.

The exercise — How many can be wrong before the decision stops meaning anything?

06The Great Filter

The accountability frame — The filter is not an asteroid. It is a species that builds systems it cannot stop.

The counter-argument — A kill switch nobody knows about is not a kill switch.

The exercise — If you cannot fill in the four fields, you found the gap.
