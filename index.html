How to use it

Deal it. Work it. Commit to three.

1

Take the frames in any order

Each stands alone: a claim, an exercise on a real system, and where the frame fails.

2

Give the counter-argument its hearing

Every frame has an obvious objection. Run it fairly, then note where the frame fails.

3

Fill in the four fields

The filter frame ends practical: who can stop it, what triggers the stop, who is told, what happens to work in progress.
